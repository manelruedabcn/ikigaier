How do you walkthrough life?

Five minutes to see yourself from the outside: how you decide, how you relate to others, how you keep your pace and how you read the ground.

Take the free test

5 minutes · no sign-up · instant result

Why start here

You may not know it, but you’ve been walking far from yourself for a while

You’ve spent years working out how to meet expectations better. You almost never stop to look at how you walk. PASO is a short mirror: it doesn’t tell you who you are, it shows you how you’re moving right now.

It’s the first step on the IKIGAIER path, the same one the books and workshops follow.

What you discover

Four questions about the way you walk

  • 01

    How you decide

    Whether you choose from what you want or from what’s expected of you.

  • 02

    How you relate to others

    How much of yourself you put into play with others, and how much you hold back.

  • 03

    How you keep your pace

    Whether your energy renews itself or wears down along the way.

  • 04

    How you read the ground

    How you look at what’s coming before taking the next step.

How it works

Three steps, five minutes

  1. Answer

    Short questions. Answer the first thing you feel, without overthinking it.

  2. See yourself

    You see your result straight away, without giving your email or creating an account.

  3. Choose

    If it resonates, you can continue the path with the letters, the books or a workshop.
