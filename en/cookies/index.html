Cookie policy

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser when you visit it. It is used to remember information about your visit, such as preferences or technical data that allow the page to work properly.

Cookies we use

IKIGAIER is a simple website that respects your privacy. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, social media pixels or profiling tools. Only the technical cookies described below may be set.

Technical cookies (strictly necessary)

Our infrastructure and security provider, Cloudflare, may set technical cookies needed to protect the site and ensure it works properly (for example, to tell legitimate traffic from automated attacks). These cookies do not collect personal information for commercial purposes and are exempt from the consent requirement under current regulations. You can find more information in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Third-party cookies

Subscription to the “IKIGAIER Letters” newsletter is handled through Substack. When you click to subscribe, you are redirected to a Substack domain, which may set its own cookies under its own responsibility. These cookies are governed by its own policy, which you can read at substack.com/cookies.

How to manage or disable cookies

You can set your browser to accept, reject or delete cookies at any time. Here are the help links for the most common browsers:
