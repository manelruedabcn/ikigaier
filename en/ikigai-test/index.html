Why another ikigai test

Ikigai doesn’t fit in four circles

Most ikigai tests repeat the four-circle diagram: what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs and what you can be paid for. In the middle, a profession. It is useful for getting your ideas in order, but it leaves out half of ikigai.

In El Ikigai que no te contaron I work with two dimensions that live side by side. Active Ikigai is the doing: direction, abilities, what you contribute. Conscious Ikigai is the being: whether you are present in what you do, whether you can be who you are, whether your life has value beyond what you achieve. An ikigaier doesn’t choose between the two. They learn to move in both.

This test explores both at once. If you want the full framework, start with what ikigai is.

What it doesn’t do

No ideal job, no percentages

It doesn’t give you a profession, an ikigai percentage or a personality label. No test can tell you what your ikigai is: what an ikigai test can tell you (and what it can’t). This one shows you where you are starting from, so that the work of recognising it begins somewhere real.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Is the ikigai test free?

Yes. It is free and you don’t need to create an account. You see your reading on screen when you finish. If you like, you can ask for it to be emailed to you.

Does this test tell me what my ikigai is?

No. No test can tell you what your ikigai is, because ikigai is not calculated: it is recognised. This test shows you where you are starting from in its two dimensions, what holds you and what deserves attention, and suggests an exercise from the book for that week.

What is it based on?

On the book El Ikigai que no te contaron, by Manel Rueda. All the questions come from its exercises, and the reading uses quotes and exercises from the book. It is not a validated psychological instrument or a diagnosis.

What are Active Ikigai and Conscious Ikigai?

Active Ikigai is the dimension of doing: direction, abilities, contribution, what you build. Conscious Ikigai is the dimension of being: presence, coherence and the sense that your life has value as it is. The test explores both.

Does it work if I don’t have paid work?

Yes. The questions refer to your main occupation, whether that is a job, caring for others, studying, looking for work or retirement.

What happens after the test?

You see your reading and, if you like, we email it to you. If you want to keep going, you can sign up for free to “A month getting to know your ikigai”: an email a week for four weeks, from the day you join, with an exercise from the book, and optional group meetings. In week four you retake the test and see what has changed.

What happens to my answers?

Closed answers are stored without personal data, to improve the test. Your email and anything you write in the open question are only stored if you ask to receive the reading by email. Receiving other communications is a separate, optional tick box. More detail in the privacy policy.
