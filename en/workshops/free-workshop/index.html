A question to begin

Do you feel something doesn’t fit, even though everything seems to be going well?

It isn’t a crisis. It’s something vaguer: a distance between how you live and how you feel you could live. That’s what this hour is about.

It isn’t a lecture or a formula. It’s another way of looking at something many people think they know: ikigai. The four-circle version stays at doing. The one nobody told you about also speaks of being.

What you’ll experience

Three acts, one path

  • I

    The Awakening

    Zombie mode, the fear that disguises itself as prudence and the masks we wear without knowing. With a live masks test.

  • II

    The Map

    The two souls of ikigai: the one that seeks direction and the one that gives roots. And why neither works on its own.

  • III

    Life in Motion

    Work, relationships, time and the conversation you have with yourself before you open your mouth.

With three short practices in the room. All you need is paper, a pen and your phone.
