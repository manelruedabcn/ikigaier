The books

One path back to yourself, in three books. You can read them in order or start with the one that calls you most today. For now, they are available in Spanish only.

1 · Release

No todo lo que te frena es tuyo

You’re not missing anything. You have too much noise.

Why a capable person can spend half a lifetime holding themselves back. The book puts on the same table two maps that are almost never read together —the shortcuts every brain comes with as standard and the beliefs installed in you as a child— and shows how one protects the other.

At the end, four tools await so that the diagnosis is yours, not mine.

For you if you’ve done what you were supposed to, and done it well, and still notice a distance between the life you lead and the person leading it.

See on Amazon
2 · Act

Disciplina para indisciplinados

Why it was never willpower

You’ve spent years collecting evidence that you’re an undisciplined person, and the conclusion is wrong. Motivation and willpower are two different systems, with different expiry dates, and nobody taught you to tell them apart.

The book helps you recognise your pattern among eight and pick a habit back up without starting from scratch every time it breaks.

For you if you’ve started a hundred Mondays and each time it’s a little harder to start again.

See on Amazon
3 · Align

El Ikigai que no te contaron

The central book of IKIGAIER

An honest look at real ikigai and the distance between what you do and who you are. It distinguishes Active Ikigai, what you build and contribute, from Conscious Ikigai, the presence with which you live it.

This book gave rise to the in-person workshop I AM IKIGAIER.

For you if you feel something doesn’t fit even though everything seems to be going well, and you want to find your way back to yourself without becoming someone else.
